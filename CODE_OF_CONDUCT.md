# Code of Conduct

Sentinel exists to make sponsored AI placements safer, more truthful, and easier
to audit. The project welcomes contributors who work in that spirit.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and
  discussions.
- Assume good intent, but be precise about evidence, tests, and public claims.
- Keep criticism focused on code, documentation, behavior, and safety impact.
- Protect private information. Do not post secrets, signing keys, private audit
  logs, sensitive user conversations, or exploit details in public channels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical or safety discussions.
- Using project channels to promote unsafe, deceptive, or manipulative ad
  placement behavior.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, decline pull requests,
or restrict participation when conduct creates safety, privacy, or collaboration
risk.

For security-sensitive reports, use the private reporting path described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.
